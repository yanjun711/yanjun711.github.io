# Institutional logos

These assets identify the institutions listed in the owner's experience.
Symbols are cropped from the original wordmarks, with their proportions
preserved, and saved as lossless WebP files. No logo artwork was generated.

- **Peking University:** red emblem from the official homepage header asset,
  https://www.pku.edu.cn/Uploads/Picture/2019/12/26/s5e04176fbbfa3.png
- **Huazhong University of Science and Technology:** emblem from the official
  visual identity website, http://vi.hust.edu.cn/imgs/logo_top.png.
  The 4000 × 4130 transparent source is preserved at `originals/hust-vi-cover.png`.
  The emblem is cropped at (1039, 0, 2961, 1457), then reduced to 512 × 388.
  The original alpha mask is rendered in the university's official blue,
  **#004B84**, for the owner's requested blue-and-white version; no geometry is
  redrawn. The color is specified in the official guide at
  http://vi.hust.edu.cn/jcbf/scgf/scxl.htm, specifically
  http://vi.hust.edu.cn/imgs/A-Part/A-3/A-3-1-1xuexiaobiaozhunse.jpg.
  The emblem specification is http://vi.hust.edu.cn/jcbf/bzgf/xhgf.htm.
  Official images were downloaded through WordPress's image proxy, for example
  https://i0.wp.com/vi.hust.edu.cn/imgs/logo_top.png. No GitHub mirror is used.
  The previously verified homepage header remains at `originals/hust-header.png`
  for provenance but is no longer the displayed asset.
- **AI² Robotics:** symbol from the official website's color logo,
  https://ai2robotics.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/logo.png
- **Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence:** symbol from the official
  homepage header asset, https://www.baai.ac.cn/img/logo.png. The original
  white symbol is rendered in black for a white background.

Saved symbol dimensions: PKU 240 × 240; HUST 512 × 388;
AI² Robotics 510 × 568; BAAI 77 × 82. All appear inside an 80 × 80 CSS pixel area
with their original aspect ratios. Source resolution is retained; no artwork
is redrawn or artificially upscaled in the saved files.
